ABOUT LIVE IN ASIATRALIA

MADE FOR THE
LIVE MUSIC
COMMUNITY.

LIVE IN ASIATRALIA is a live music information and travel platform focused on the Asia-Pacific region. Our goal is to help music fans discover concert announcements, festival news, tour dates, venues and useful travel information in one place.

Beyond concert information, we also aim to develop concert travel services that connect fans with accommodation, transport, local experiences and trip planning — so that following your favourite artist can become a memorable journey.
